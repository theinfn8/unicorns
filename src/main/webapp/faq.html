Why unicorns?

Because UNICORNS.

Why adopt Unicorns?

It is sad to see such beautiful creatures sitting on shelves or in windows just waiting for a loving home.

How many Unicorns are in your stable?

The stables can accomodate any number of Unicorns that we have a need for. There are currently 10 permanent residents, and a range of adoptable Unicorns. Typically there are anywhere from three to six Unicorns in need of a good home at any given time. Maybe you could be the next person to give one a loving home!

How does one adopt a Unicorn?

When you have identified the Unicorn you wish to adopt, there is an adoption request form that can be reached from the Unicorn's information page. Fill out the required information and hit submit. Our staff will evaluate your answers against the personality and needs of that particular Unicorn and get back to you within a week on whether they feel it will be a good fit.

After that, you are assigned an adoption representative and they will help walk you through the process of getting your new friend for life.
